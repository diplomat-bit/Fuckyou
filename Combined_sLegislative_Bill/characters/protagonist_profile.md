### II. MECHANICS & SUBTERFUGE

#### The Regulatory Loophole: Regulation J & UCC Article 9-312
Vance’s architecture exploits a critical vulnerability in the intersection of Federal Reserve Regulation J (Subpart B - Funds Transfers Through Fedwire) and Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) Article 9-312, which governs the priority of security interests in deposit accounts.

Under traditional banking operations, municipal tax revenues sit in commercial "custodial accounts" for up to 45 days before being disbursed for public works. During this settlement window, commercial banks rehypothecate these funds, lending them out at high overnight rates while paying the municipality 0.05% interest.

This classification allows the funds to bypass the commercial custodial accounts entirely. Instead, they are routed into a decentralized, algorithmic liquidity pool managed by the MTLS AI.

#### The Legacy Failure & The AI Superiority
