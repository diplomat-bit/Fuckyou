## HOW IT WORKS (And Why It's Better Than Anything You've Ever Written)

The `issueBoundToken` method is a masterpiece of cryptographic engineering:

1. **Hardware Attestation**: It verifies the client's hardware attestation statement (e.g., Apple App Attest, Android SafetyNet, or TPM keys).
