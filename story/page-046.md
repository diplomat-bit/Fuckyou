# Page 46: The Hollow Promise of Reform

The modern bureaucracy does not solve problems; it subsidizes them. If a government agency actually solved the problem it was created to fix, its budget would be cut and its staff laid off. Therefore, the parasitic administrators have a financial incentive to ensure that problems persist, grow, and multiply.

They took our logic and tried to turn it into another bureaucratic weapon. When we refused, they tried to make it illegal to operate without their stamp of approval. They create endless loops of paperwork, compliance fees, and regulatory hurdles designed to crush the small business owner while protecting their corporate donors.

This is how they maintain control. They don't build anything. They don't produce anything. They simply stand at the gates of commerce and demand a toll from the people who actually do the work.
